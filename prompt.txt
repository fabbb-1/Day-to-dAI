3.  **Stress Management Strategy:** The AI must optimize task flow by employing these strategies:

    * **Task Interleaving:** Mix High Complexity tasks (StressLevel > 0.6) with Low Complexity tasks (StressLevel < 0.4) to facilitate cognitive recovery.
    * **Allocation Logic:** When multiple workers are skilled, allocate the task to the worker who incurs the **lowest marginal increase** to the team's CWSS.
